FAQs

Quick answers about ICCHA’s pre-launch, orders, shipping, sizing, and support.

Orders & Pre-Launch

Is ICCHA launched yet?

ICCHA is currently in a pre-launch phase. You can join the waitlist to get early updates when the first collection goes live.

Can I place an order right now?

Not yet. Once the first collection is available, the Products page will show pricing, sizes, and checkout.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You’ll receive updates about product drops, early access, and launch announcements. No spam—unsubscribe anytime.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

Shipping regions will be confirmed at launch. We’ll publish a full shipping page with delivery timelines and charges.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery timelines depend on location and service level. At launch, we’ll show estimated delivery dates during checkout.

Will there be free shipping?

We may offer free shipping promotions during launch periods or for minimum order values. Details will be announced.

Returns & Exchanges

Do you accept returns?

Our return and exchange policy will be shared at launch. We aim to keep it simple, fair, and customer-first.

What if the size doesn’t fit?

We plan to support size exchanges (subject to stock availability). Our sizing guide will help you pick correctly.

What items can’t be returned?

For hygiene and safety, worn items or products without original packaging may not be eligible. Full terms at launch.

Products & Sizing

How do I choose my size?

We’ll provide a size chart with measurements and fit notes for each product. If you’re between sizes, we’ll guide you.

Are your clothes good for daily wear?

Yes—ICCHA is designed for everyday comfort and style: workdays, outings, and every occasion in between.

Will you release new colors or styles?

New drops and colorways may arrive over time. Join the waitlist to get early access updates.

Account & Support

Do I need an account to buy?

We may allow guest checkout. Creating an account helps you track orders and manage preferences.

How can I contact ICCHA?

You can use the Contact page or the Feedback form. We’ll publish official support details at launch.

I found a bug on the website—what should I do?

Please share it via the Feedback page with a short description and screenshot if possible. It helps us improve faster.
